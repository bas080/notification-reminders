Notification Reminders is a lightweight, open-source Android application that monitors incoming device notifications and intelligently matches them against your custom active reminders.

### Key Features
- **Smart Notification Monitoring**: Automatically monitors incoming notifications in the background.
- **Intelligent Keyword Matching**: Case-insensitive word matching that filters out common stop-words and matches keywords anywhere within incoming messages.
- **Quick Reminder Management**: Easily add reminders directly in the app or quick-add inline straight from your notification shade.
- **Grouped Reminder Alerts**: Matched reminders are neatly organized together in your notification center with one-tap action to mark complete.
- **Privacy First & Lightweight**: Free and open source with zero ads, zero analytics, zero trackers, and no external servers.
- **In-App Activity Logs**: Built-in visual log viewer to inspect application activity anytime.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Clean, text-first user interface that seamlessly follows system themes.
